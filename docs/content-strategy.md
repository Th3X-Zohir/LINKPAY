## 6. FAQ Section

**Section Headline:** Questions Freelancers Ask Us

---

### Q1: Is LinkPay legal in Bangladesh?

**A:** Yes, 100%. LinkPay BD operates through our payment gateway partner **aamarPay**, which is a fully licensed Payment Service Provider (PSP) registered with Bangladesh Bank. We don't handle money directly — all transactions go through aamarPay's regulated infrastructure. This is the same standard used by major businesses in Bangladesh.

---

### Q2: How long does it take to receive money?

**A:** Once your client completes the payment on our secure page, the funds typically arrive in your **bKash account within 24 hours** (or 24–48 hours for bank transfers). We process payouts daily at 5:00 PM BDT. Your dashboard shows real-time status updates at every step.

---

### Q3: What cards can my clients use?

**A:** Your international clients can pay with **any Visa or Mastercard** — credit or debit. This includes cards from the US, UK, Europe, Canada, Australia, and 180+ other countries. Your client doesn't need a Bangladeshi account. They just need a card.

---

### Q4: What are the fees?

**A:** Simple and transparent:

- **Platform fee:** 0.75% per transaction (you keep 99.25%)
- **aamarPay gateway fee:** ~2.25% (charged by the payment gateway, not us)

**Example:** For a $500 payment (≈৳55,000):
- aamarPay fee: ৳1,238
- LinkPay fee: ৳413
- **You receive: ৳53,349**

Compare this to Payoneer (2–3% + withdrawal) or Wise (1.5% + conversion markup).

---

### Q5: Do I need a bank account?

**A:** No. You can receive payouts directly to your **bKash account** — no bank account required. We also support Nagad and major Bangladeshi banks. Your bKash number is all you need to get started.

---

### Q6: How is this different from Payoneer or Wise?

**A:** Here's the honest comparison:

| Feature | Payoneer | Wise | LinkPay BD |
|---------|----------|------|------------|
| Setup time | 3–7 days | 1–2 days | 30 seconds |
| Platform fee | 1–2% | 0.5–1% | **0.75%** |
| Gateway fee | + withdrawal | + conversion | **Only 0.75% total** |
| Payout to bKash | 2–3 days | 1–2 days | **24 hours** |
| Client experience | "Send me your Payoneer link" | "Use my Wise email" | **Professional payment link** |
| Invoice/branding | Basic | Basic | **Custom branded invoices (Premium)** |

The key difference: **Your client pays you with a card, you receive money in bKash. No intermediary account needed.**
