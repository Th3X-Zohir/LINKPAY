1. Executive Summary
LinkPay BD is a mobile-first web + app platform that lets Bangladeshi freelancers create and send a professional payment link in under 30 seconds.
Client pays with international card → money lands in our aamarPay merchant account → we take only 0.75% platform fee → rest goes straight to freelancer’s bKash or bank in 1–2 days.
Why it wins:

PayPal is still not working in Bangladesh (only “coming soon” announcements).
Existing options (Payoneer, Wise, SSLCOMMERZ direct) feel complicated and expensive.
LinkPay is simpler, cheaper, and made for Bangladeshis.

Business Model: Aggregator (we hold one aamarPay merchant account) + freemium.
Startup Cost: Only ৳25,000–40,000 (gateway setup + domain + basic hosting).
Year 1 Revenue Target: ৳18–25 lakh
Year 3 Revenue Target: ৳1.2–1.8 crore
Profit Margin: 55–65% after all costs.
2. Market Opportunity (Huge & Growing)

Active freelancers in Bangladesh: 650,000+ (second-largest in the world).
Annual forex earnings from freelancing: $500–800 million.
Expected growth: 750,000–1 million freelancers by end of 2026.
Big pain: No easy “send link → client pays with card” tool that settles directly to local bKash/bank without heavy KYC or high fees.

Target Customers
Primary: Freelancers (web developers, graphic designers, writers, digital marketers, video editors) who work with direct international clients.
Secondary: Small agencies, coaches, YouTubers, and anyone selling digital services abroad.
Market Size for Us
Even if we capture just 1% of 650,000 freelancers = 6,500 users.
Average user processes ৳1.5–2 lakh/month → huge opportunity.
3. Problem & Solution
Problem
Freelancers waste time and money:

Payoneer/Wise = complicated approvals + 2–3% + withdrawal hassle.
Direct SSLCOMMERZ/aamarPay = each person needs own merchant account + heavy documents.
Clients hate “send me your Payoneer link” — looks unprofessional.

Our Solution
One simple dashboard:

User logs in → enters amount + project description.
System creates beautiful invoice + payment link (via aamarPay API).
Share via WhatsApp/Email/Facebook.
Client pays with Visa/Mastercard (no account needed).
Money arrives → we deduct 0.75% → auto-payout to user’s bKash/bank.

Extra free features: QR code, payment reminders, basic invoice PDF.
